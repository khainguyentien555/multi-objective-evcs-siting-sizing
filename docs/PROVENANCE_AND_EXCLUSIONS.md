# Provenance and exclusions

## Included from the private GTSD working archive

- `References/Result_3 Methods_New.xlsx` → `data/source/result_3_methods_new.xlsx`.
- Author-created spatial-network and workflow figure assets used for technical inspection.
- Paper-facing numerical values transcribed from the final camera-ready six-page PDF into machine-readable CSV files.

## Deliberately excluded

- Camera-ready DOCX/PDF and publisher/submission summaries.
- Conference program booklet and banner assets.
- Presentation deck, presentation PDF, and event photographs.
- Third-party literature PDFs, RIS/EndNote libraries, reviewer/reference working files.
- Stock EV icons and their license files.
- Third-party/background site imagery and map composites used for presentation graphics.
- EndNote database internals (`GTSD2026.Data`).
- Developmental solver source from the broader thesis archive that is not exact-formulation-identical to the camera-ready conference model.

The exclusion policy keeps the public repository focused on reproducible numerical evidence and avoids presenting third-party or noncanonical material as part of the scientific source of truth.
